想被认真对待的恋爱体验

Love stories that take you seriously
Collar × Malice Steam · Switch

犯罪悬疑包裹的恋爱，每个角色都有真实的内心创伤，不会因为你是女主就对你客气。

Romance wrapped in crime thriller — every route carries real emotional weight, not just charm.

Bustafellows Steam · Switch · iOS

如果你想要一个真正把你当主角写的女主，而不是背景板，选这个。

A heroine written as an actual protagonist — sharp, funny, and never passive.

只想安静待一会儿

Quiet games for quiet moments
A Short Hike Steam · Switch

不恋爱，不打 Boss，就是爬山和遇见人。治愈到离谱，两小时结束，刚刚好。

No combat, no stakes — just hiking and small conversations. Over in two hours, and that's perfect.

Spiritfarer Steam · Switch

关于告别的游戏。玩完会哭，但不是那种让你难受的哭。

A game about letting go. You will cry, but it's the kind that feels like relief.

Coffee Talk Steam · iOS

深夜咖啡馆，听别人说话，冲饮料，不需要做任何重要决定。

Late-night café, strangers' stories, drinks to brew. Nothing is urgent here.

想要有点分量的故事

Stories with real weight
Disco Elysium Steam

目前叙事密度最高的 RPG 之一。没有战斗，全靠对话和内心独白驱动，女性玩家评价极高。

The densest narrative RPG ever made. No combat — just dialogue, choices, and a detective falling apart.

The Forgotten City Steam

罗马时代的时间循环推理。全程靠对话和智慧通关，没有一场战斗是必须打的。

Time-loop mystery in ancient Rome. Every puzzle solved through conversation, never combat.

想要真实的群像和关系

Ensemble casts worth caring about
Hades Steam · Switch

打架之外是一群有血有肉的神。关系网络比主线更好看，每次死亡都推进剧情。

The combat is excellent; the cast of gods is better. Every run deepens a relationship.

Strange Horticulture Steam

一个人经营草药铺，和小镇居民慢慢建立信任，气氛是那种阴天下午的安静。

Run a herbalist shop, slowly earn the town's trust. Quiet and atmospheric like a grey afternoon.
